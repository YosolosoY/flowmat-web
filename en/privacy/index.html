FlowMat

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · Versión en español

In short: the data you record in FlowMat (your students, their medical conditions, your classes and your sales) is yours. If you sign in, it is saved to your account so you don't lose it, and only you can see it. We have no ads, we don't sell data and we don't track what you do.

Who is responsible

FlowMat is an app developed by Mateo Monsalve (Chile). For any privacy question, write to mateo.monsalve@gmail.com.

What data you record in the app

FlowMat is a tool for pilates teachers. What you record may include:

  • About your students: name, age or date of birth, phone, level, goals, medical conditions (for example injuries, pregnancy or osteoporosis) with their details, medical clearance date, emergency contact, treating doctor and your notes.
  • Your groups, places, class cycles, scheduled classes, attendance and the classes you built.
  • Your sales and payments: prices, installments, dates and payment methods (no card data).
  • Your settings: plans, payment methods, currency, favorite exercises, language.

You decide what to record. We recommend having your students' consent to store their health data, and recording only what is needed to keep them safe in class.

Where it is stored

  • Without an account: everything stays only on your phone. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, it is lost (unless you exported a backup file).
  • With an account (Google sign-in): a copy is also saved in the cloud, so you can restore it on another phone. We store your email, your name, your Google account identifier and the (compressed) copy of your data. The copy is stored with Supabase, our provider, on servers in São Paulo, Brazil, over an encrypted connection. Each account can only read its own data.

We never sell this data or use it for advertising. We don't look at it unless you ask us to help you with a problem.

Purchases

Purchases in FlowMat are processed by Google Play. We don't see or store your card data. To know what you bought we use RevenueCat, which receives from Google Play the purchase information (product, date and status) linked to an identifier of your account.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a technical report to Sentry so we can fix it: which error happened, on which screen, the app version and the phone model and system. It does not include your name, your email, your students' data or anything you type in the app.

Notifications and WhatsApp

The daily reminder with your classes is scheduled by your own phone; it doesn't go through any server. WhatsApp reminders only open WhatsApp with a written message: you decide whether to send it, and FlowMat doesn't receive or store those conversations.

Health

FlowMat suggests exercises based on the conditions you record, but it does not replace a health professional's judgment. If you have any doubt about a student, ask their doctor.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools (the crash report is only sent when something fails).
  • We don't access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We don't share your data or your students' data with anyone.

How to delete your data

  • Data on the phone: delete students, cycles or sales in the app, use More → Your account → "Sign out and erase this phone's data", or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and the cloud copy: in the app, go to More → Your account → Delete my account. They are deleted immediately. You can also ask by email; see how to delete your account.

Minors

FlowMat is for adult teachers; it is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is important, we will also let you know in the app.
