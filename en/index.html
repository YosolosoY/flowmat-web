Frequently asked questions

Who is FlowMat for?

For pilates teachers who give private or group classes, on their own or in a studio. It isn't an app for students to train alone.

Does it cover the reformer?

For now FlowMat covers mat, chair and wall, with equipment like band, weights, ring, small ball, fitball, foam roller and ankle weights. The reformer is planned for a future version.

Does it replace medical clearance?

No. FlowMat helps you plan, but it doesn't replace a health professional's judgment. That's why it stores the date of the medical clearance and reminds you when it's about to expire.

What about group classes?

The group's most restrictive condition rules. If some students didn't come, you can rebuild the class with only those present. Group classes are part of FlowMat full.

Can I change the class it builds?

Yes: swap an exercise, the order or the reps, add or remove. You can also mark favorite exercises and others you never want it to propose.

What if I lose my phone?

If you signed in with your Google account, everything is backed up automatically. Without an account, the data stays only on the phone; you can also export a backup file.

Do I need internet?

Not to build and teach classes. Internet is used to back up your data to your account and to buy FlowMat full.

Is there an iPhone version?

For now FlowMat is Android only.

Important: FlowMat helps you plan classes, but it doesn't replace a health professional's judgment. If you have any doubt about a student, check with their doctor.
