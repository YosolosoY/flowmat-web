Guidance for the class

Protect your ankle: avoid jumps and impact, limit squats to 90°, and keep a soft knee when landing.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulAnkle pumps and circles

Reduce the range of motion and avoid wide circles at first; progress as tolerated.

CarefulStep touch

Control the side step to avoid the ankle's inversion mechanism.

CarefulStanding knee to elbow

Hold onto a chair or wall to keep the standing ankle stable.

CarefulLow-impact jacks

Control the landing as the foot returns to center; avoid this if the ankle is freshly injured.

CarefulButt kicks

Reduce impact and avoid prolonged single-leg weight-bearing; skip it if there's acute pain.

CarefulSide leg lift

Reduce the range of motion or hold onto a wall if you feel unstable.

CarefulThe Bug

Distribute weight evenly between both feet and avoid balancing only on the toes.

CarefulChest Expansion

Lift the heels gradually and hold onto a chair if you feel unstable.

CarefulCharlie Chaplin

Move between flexed and pointed feet gently, without forcing the ankle.

CarefulStanding Balance with Opposite Knee and Arm

Hold a chair or wall and limit knee height if the ankle feels unstable.

CarefulStanding leg swing

Keep the standing foot stable and reduce the range if your ankle feels unstable.

CarefulJumping jacks

Do the low-impact variation (Step Jack): tap one foot out to the side without jumping, return to the center and alternate sides.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with ankle (recent sprain or instability)?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with ankle (recent sprain or instability) and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
