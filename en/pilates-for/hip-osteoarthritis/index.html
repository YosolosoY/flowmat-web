Pilates and hip osteoarthritis

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with hip osteoarthritis. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Work in pain-free ranges and avoid putting a lot of weight on the affected hip.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulFloor Adductor Stretch with Band

Open the leg only until you feel a gentle stretch, without forcing the hip joint.

CarefulCross-Legged Forward Bend

If you feel hip discomfort, loosen the leg cross or sit on a higher cushion.

CarefulReclined Butterfly

Rest the knees on cushions if you feel tightness or discomfort in the hip.

CarefulKneeling hip flexor stretch

Do the stretch gently, without forcing the end range of the hip.

CarefulAdductor lift with bent top knee

Place a cushion under the top knee if the bent position feels uncomfortable.

CarefulAlternating knee to chest

Bring the knee to the chest only as far as the hip feels pain-free.

CarefulStanding leg swing

Work within a small, pain-free range and don't force the end of the swing.

CarefulKneeling hip stretch with relaxed arms

Work within a pain-free range and use a cushion if your hip doesn't tolerate full flexion.

CarefulSide Kick Circles

Keep the circles small and within a pain-free hip range.

CarefulSide Kick Staggered Legs

Reduce how high you lift the top leg if you feel hip discomfort.

CarefulKneeling Side Kicks

Lower the height and range of the kick if your support hip hurts.

CarefulSide Kick Double-Leg Lift

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with hip osteoarthritis?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with hip osteoarthritis and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
