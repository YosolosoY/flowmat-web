Pilates and postpartum after C-section

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with postpartum after C-section. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Skip flexed-spine ab series; cue el abdomen profundo activation with breath and keep her head supported until neutral trunk-pelvis control is achieved.

How the class is adjusted

  • Reps and times at 70% of normal.
  • 20 seconds of rest between exercises.
  • It asks for medical clearance before starting.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with postpartum after C-section?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with postpartum after C-section and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
