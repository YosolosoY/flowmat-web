Guidance for the class

Avoid holding your breath, intense effort, and lying face down; stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, as advised by your doctor.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with pregnancy (first trimester)?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with pregnancy (first trimester) and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
