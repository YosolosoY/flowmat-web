Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulKnee to chest

Hug only one knee and do not rock with both knees together.

CarefulWall roll down

Roll your spine down slowly without extra load, keeping your abs engaged and avoiding excessive rounding.

CarefulAssisted roll up

Use your hands only as light support and avoid lowering past mid-back.

CarefulSingle leg stretch

Keep your abs engaged and avoid letting your lower back lift off the mat.

CarefulSpine stretch forward

Round forward from the head, keeping your lower back long and stable, without forcing the stretch.

CarefulSwan prep

Control the range of motion and avoid excessive compression in the lower back.

CarefulBridge with ring squeeze

Lift and lower as one solid piece, without rolling through the spine, avoiding lower back flexion.

CarefulRoll back with ball

Use the ball only for support and avoid loading your lower back with extra resistance.

CarefulChest opening on foam roller

Cover the roller with a towel or thin mat and shorten the time on it if you feel bone discomfort.

CarefulThe Bug

Keep the back flat as you fold forward, never rounding the spine.

CarefulStanding Side Bends

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with osteoporosis or osteopenia?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with osteoporosis or osteopenia and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
