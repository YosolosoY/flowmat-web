Guidance for the class

Protect your knee: don't squat past 90°, keep it soft and aligned with your toes, and land gently. Stop if you feel discomfort.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulStep touch

Watch that the knee doesn't collapse inward during the side step.

CarefulStanding knee to elbow

Bring your knee toward your elbow gently, without any jolt or impact.

CarefulShoulder bridge

In the single-leg variation, keep the thigh aligned with the hip without rotating it.

CarefulMermaid

Slightly separate your legs instead of keeping your shins together.

CarefulSingle leg balance

Use nearby support and keep the knee well aligned; stop if discomfort appears.

CarefulThe Bug

Keep the knees soft and aligned with the feet, without bending too deeply.

CarefulIT Band Release with Roller

Don't roll over the knee joint; stop if you feel sharp pain.

CarefulCharlie Chaplin

Reduce pressure on the ring if you feel knee discomfort when bending.

CarefulBand Kickback

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with knee (ligaments / meniscus / chondromalacia)?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with knee (ligaments / meniscus / chondromalacia) and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
