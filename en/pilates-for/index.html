Pilates by condition

Every condition has its own rules in FlowMat. Pick one to see which exercises are not suggested, which come with a warning and how the class is adjusted.

Spine and back

  • Low back pain (no herniation)
  • Lumbar disc herniation or bulge
  • Sciatica
  • Spondylolysis or spondylolisthesis
  • Lumbar spinal stenosis
  • Scoliosis
  • Hyperkyphosis
  • Lumbar hyperlordosis
  • Neck pain or cervical herniation

Bones and joints

  • Osteoporosis or osteopenia
  • Knee osteoarthritis
  • Hip osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Shoulder (rotator cuff / impingement / frozen shoulder)
  • Wrist or carpal tunnel
  • Knee (ligaments / meniscus / chondromalacia)
  • Ankle (recent sprain or instability)

Joint replacements

  • Hip replacement (posterior approach)
  • Hip replacement (anterior approach)
  • Knee replacement

Pregnancy and postpartum

  • Pregnancy (first trimester)
  • Pregnancy (second and third trimester)
  • Postpartum (vaginal birth)
  • Postpartum after C-section
  • Diastasis recti
  • Weak pelvic floor / incontinence / prolapse

Heart, blood pressure and metabolism

  • High blood pressure
  • Heart disease
  • Low blood pressure
  • Varicose veins or venous insufficiency
  • Diabetes
  • Obesity

Breathing and nervous system

  • Asthma or COPD
  • Vertigo or dizziness
  • Migraine
  • Parkinson's or another neurological disease

Other conditions

  • Glaucoma / retinal detachment / high myopia
  • Abdominal or inguinal hernia
  • Recent abdominal surgery
  • Fibromyalgia
  • Cancer treatment
  • Lymphedema
Important: FlowMat helps plan classes, but it does not replace a health professional’s judgment. If in doubt about a student, ask their treating doctor.
