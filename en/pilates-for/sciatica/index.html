Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulWall roll down

Keep your knees slightly bent and don't force the stretch if you feel tension down your leg.

CarefulAssisted roll up

Shorten the range and stop if you feel tingling or pulling down your leg.

CarefulSwan prep

Keep the abdominals engaged and limit the chest lift if you feel symptoms down the leg.

CarefulDead bug

Keep both knees bent in tabletop; if you feel pain or shooting down the leg, stop the movement.

CarefulSpine twist

Use a small range, avoid flexing the torso at the same time, and stop if pain radiates down the leg.

CarefulSeated row with band

If you feel sciatic tension, bend the knees slightly or rest them on a cushion.

CarefulSupine leg press with band

Avoid bringing the knee too close to the chest; work in a comfortable range without pulling on the leg.

CarefulThe Bug

If you feel pain or tingling down the leg, reduce the forward lean of the torso.

CarefulCross-Legged Forward Bend

Bend your knees slightly and stop leaning forward if pain shoots down your leg.

CarefulSupine marching

Lower only as far as pain-free with no leg irradiation; reduce the range if discomfort appears.

CarefulSeated roll back with raised arms

Stop the roll-back halfway and bend your knees more if your low back feels uncomfortable.

CarefulRolling preparation

Stop if you feel pain radiating into the leg and reduce the range.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with sciatica?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with sciatica and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
