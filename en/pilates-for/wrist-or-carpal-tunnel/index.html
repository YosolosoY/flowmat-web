Pilates and wrist or carpal tunnel

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with wrist or carpal tunnel. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Avoid bearing weight on the hands with the wrist bent: rest on the forearms or fists.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with wrist or carpal tunnel?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with wrist or carpal tunnel and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
