Pilates and fibromyalgia

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with fibromyalgia. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Work in short blocks with passive rests, avoid fast paces, and stop if pain appears or you lose your breath.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

How the class is adjusted

  • Reps and times at 60% of normal.
  • 45 seconds of rest between exercises.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with fibromyalgia?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with fibromyalgia and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
