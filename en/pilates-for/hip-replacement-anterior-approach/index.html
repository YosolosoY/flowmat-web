Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulSwan prep

Limit hip extension if you're in an early post-surgical phase; check with your doctor.

CarefulClam

Limit the knee opening and avoid wide external rotation; reduce the range further if in early post-surgical phase.

CarefulFigure-4 stretch

Avoid this position if your surgery was less than 12 months ago; afterward, do it seated and assisted only, not past 100°.

CarefulThe Bug

Avoid leaning too deeply forward; keep the knee soft as you bend.

CarefulIT Band Release with Roller

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with hip replacement (anterior approach)?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with hip replacement (anterior approach) and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
