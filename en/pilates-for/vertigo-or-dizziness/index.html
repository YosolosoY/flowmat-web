Pilates and vertigo or dizziness

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with vertigo or dizziness. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Avoid balance exercises or quick movements without support; use a chair or other support and progress only once you feel stable.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulDiaphragmatic breathing

Do only 2-3 cycles and stop if you feel dizzy, leaving a longer pause between breaths.

CarefulGentle neck mobility

Keep the nod in a short range, without tilting the head back or forcing the chin.

CarefulCat-cow

Keep the head more aligned with the spine, without letting it fully drop; do fewer reps or pause if you feel dizzy.

CarefulMarching in place

Hold onto a wall or chair, lower your knee height, and avoid closing your eyes or turning your head.

CarefulStanding knee to elbow

Use support (chair or wall) or reduce the twisting range if you feel dizzy.

CarefulThe Bug

Keep the head aligned with the spine and avoid sudden movements as you lean forward.

CarefulChest Expansion

Turn your head slowly and hold onto a wall if you feel unsteady.

CarefulStanding Side Bends

Use a nearby support or wall if you feel unsteady while bending.

CarefulKneeling hip flexor stretch

Use a stable support and avoid sudden movements entering or exiting the stretch.

CarefulLooking at the stars pose

Raise your gaze very slowly and use a wall or chair for support if you feel unsteady.

CarefulGratitude focused breathing

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with vertigo or dizziness?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with vertigo or dizziness and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
