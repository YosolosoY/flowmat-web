Pilates and cancer treatment

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with cancer treatment. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Adjust the intensity to each day's fatigue; when very tired, do only mobility and breathing. If new bone pain, fever or dizziness appear, stop the class and consult their medical team.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulIT Band Release with Roller

Check with your doctor and use gentle pressure; avoid rolling over treated or sensitive areas.

CarefulTrapezius release with balls

Use very gentle pressure and stop if you feel pain or skin sensitivity.

How the class is adjusted

  • Reps and times at 70% of normal.
  • 30 seconds of rest between exercises.
  • It asks for medical clearance before starting.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with cancer treatment?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with cancer treatment and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
