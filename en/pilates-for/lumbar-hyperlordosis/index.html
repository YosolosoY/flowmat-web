Guidance for the class

Engage your core and squeeze your glutes before extending hips or back; if your low back arches, lower the range or rest on your knees.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulSingle leg circle

Keep the lower back neutral and make the circle smaller if the pelvis or low back start moving.

CarefulBird dog

Keep the pelvis stable and avoid arching the lower back when extending the leg.

CarefulShoulder bridge

Don't lift the hips too high; if you feel lower back tension, lower the bridge and engage glutes and abs.

CarefulHeel slides

Place a hand under your lower back to feel if it arches, and reduce the range if it does.

CarefulKneeling plank

Draw your navel toward your spine to keep your low back from sagging.

CarefulSide-lying quad stretch

Keep a slight posterior pelvic tilt and draw your tailbone toward the bottom leg throughout the stretch.

CarefulBridge with ring squeeze

Limit how high you lift the bridge and avoid arching your back; squeeze your glutes.

CarefulBridge with ball squeeze

Don't lift too high; form a straight line and avoid arching your lower back.

CarefulThe Bug

Engage your abdomen before leaning forward so the back doesn't arch.

CarefulChest Expansion

Engage the abdomen so the low back doesn't arch as you open the chest back.

CarefulBand Kickback

Engage your abs before extending the leg to avoid arching the lower back.

CarefulBent-Knee Hip Lift with Band

Engage your abs and glutes before lifting the thigh to avoid arching your lower back.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with lumbar hyperlordosis?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with lumbar hyperlordosis and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
