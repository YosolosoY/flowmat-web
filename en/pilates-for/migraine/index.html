Guidance for the class

Avoid Swimming-style hyperextensions and cues like 'lift your chest' or 'squeeze shoulder blades'; guide diaphragmatic breathing into the back to release neck-upper back tension.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with migraine?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with migraine and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
