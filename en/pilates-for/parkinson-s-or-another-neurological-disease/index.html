Pilates and parkinson's or another neurological disease

This is how FlowMat builds pilates classes for a student with parkinson's or another neurological disease. They are the app's real rules, reviewed by a medical committee: what isn't recommended is never suggested, and what needs care comes with a warning for the teacher.

Guidance for the class

Work close to a support, avoid quick changes of position and allow extra time to get down to and up from the floor.

Exercises FlowMat does not suggest

No exercise is ruled out for this condition: the care is in how each one is done. FlowMat suggests them with a warning and, when one exists, a gentler variation.

Exercises that come with a warning

Examples of exercises that do appear, with the guidance the teacher gets.

CarefulMarching in place

Rest your hands on a fixed surface and lower your knee lift to reduce the risk of falling.

CarefulStanding Balance with Opposite Knee and Arm

Practice next to a firm support and allow extra time to find your balance.

CarefulTall Kneeling Balance with Extended Arms

Do the exercise near a wall or chair so you can steady yourself if you lose balance.

CarefulSingle Leg Pulses with Ring

Stay close to a firm support and slow the pulses if stability is limited.

CarefulSingle Leg Lowers with Ring

Keep a support nearby and reduce the range if balance is challenging.

CarefulFront splits

Do the exercise near support and at a slow pace to protect your balance.

CarefulMarching on foam roller

Move very slowly and press your hands down beside you to stop the roller.

CarefulStanding hamstring curl with chair support

Keep both hands on the chair back throughout and allow extra time between reps.

CarefulSeated Balance Without Backrest

Place the chair near a wall or support and keep your hands close to the seat for safety.

CarefulStanding Knee Raise with Wall Support

Work very close to the wall with firm support and allow extra time to find your balance.

CarefulStanding Wall Dead Bug

Stay close to the wall and rest the foot on the floor between reps if needed.

CarefulWall half-moon pose

Use the wall as constant support and shorten the hold if you feel unstable.

How the class is adjusted

  • Reps and times at 80% of normal.
  • 30 seconds of rest between exercises.
  • It asks for medical clearance before starting.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do pilates with parkinson's or another neurological disease?

It depends on each person and on what their treating doctor says. FlowMat does not replace that judgment: it helps the teacher plan the class, avoiding what isn't recommended with parkinson's or another neurological disease and flagging what needs care.

What does FlowMat do with exercises that are not recommended?

It never suggests them when building the class, and they don’t show up as a replacement when you swap an exercise either.

Can I change the class FlowMat builds?

Yes. You can swap, remove, add or reorder exercises and adjust the reps. The catalog only offers exercises that are safe for the student.
