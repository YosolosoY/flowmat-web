Delete my FlowMat account

FlowMat (Android app) · Versión en español

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open FlowMat and go to the More tab.
  2. Under Your account, tap your name and then Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and the backup copy in your Google Drive are deleted right away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to lince.revision@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FlowMat account", from the same Google email you signed in with. We delete it within 7 days at most and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name and Google identifier.
  • The backup copy in your Google Drive (students with their medical conditions, groups, programs, classes, notes and settings). When you delete the account from the app, FlowMat removes that file. If you ask for deletion by email, we can only delete your account; you remove the hidden file yourself from your Google account settings (in the section for third-party apps and services connected to your account, find FlowMat and choose to delete its hidden data).

What is on your phone is not deleted with the account; it is deleted when you uninstall the app or with "Sign out and erase this phone's data".

What is kept

If you bought something in FlowMat, Google Play keeps the purchase record under its own policies, and RevenueCat keeps the transaction history for as long as the law requires (for example, for refunds or taxes).
